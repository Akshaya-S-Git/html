A small story

Extra step

Riya was learning to ride a bicycle.At first, she kept losing her balance and falling. She thought, “Maybe I am not good at this.” But instead of giving up, she practiced for a few minutes every day. One evening, she finally rode across the street without stopping. She looked back and realized that all those small attempts had helped her improve. She smiled and said,
“I didn't become better in one day. I became better by trying every day”.

Lesson: Small progress is still progress.
